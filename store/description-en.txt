Control your own Music Assistant server from SailfishOS — no cloud involved. Not affiliated with the Music Assistant project; the name belongs to its authors.

REMOTE CONTROL

- Every player of your setup at a glance, with what is playing, progress and play/pause in the row.
- A page per player with artwork, title, seekable progress, next/previous and a volume slider that follows your finger. The background takes on the colours of the artwork.
- Radio shows "Live"; audiobooks and podcasts get chapters, −15 s/+30 s skips and playback speed.
- Lyrics that follow the song, and a sleep timer that runs on the server.
- Queue: jump, reorder, shuffle, repeat, hand it to another player, save it as a playlist.
- Group speakers, with a group volume and a slider per speaker.
- Announcements: type a text, the speaker says it.
- Cover view and lock screen control the player without opening the app.

LIBRARY

- Artists, albums, tracks, playlists, radio, podcasts and audiobooks, searchable and filterable by favourites; plus "Recently played" and "Continue listening".
- Search the library and connected services, or the library only.
- Artists with popular tracks and similar artists; "Play similar" builds an endless queue of matching tracks.
- Podcasts like in a podcatcher: date, "new", "43 % listened", description.
- Audiobooks with progress, resume or start over, and a chapter list.
- Edit playlists, add results to your library, add radio stations from the worldwide directory or by stream address.

EVERYDAY

- Changes made elsewhere show up immediately.
- A second server address for away from home (VPN, Tailscale, reverse proxy); at home it is never used.
- Artwork is cached on the device, saving mobile data.
- Demo mode to try it without a server of your own.
- German and English; portrait orientation can be locked.

REQUIREMENTS

- A Music Assistant server you can reach (default port 8095). If it runs as a Home Assistant app, that is the address of the HA machine, not the ingress address.
- An access token, created in the Music Assistant interface under Settings → Profile.

Address and token are stored encrypted in Sailfish Secrets and tied to the device lock.

A NOTE ON THE AUDIO PERMISSION

The app asks for the Audio permission although it neither records nor plays anything. On SailfishOS that is the permission which allows registering an MPRIS service — that is, the controls on the lock screen. There is no finer-grained way to get there.
